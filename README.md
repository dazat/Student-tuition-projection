# Student-tuition-projection
Calculating student tuition and projecting future tuition income based on student characteristics and course of study differences in tuition costs.

The goal is to aid our university in calculating tuition costs for students and make predictions about future income for the university as we work to implement strategic planning for the future and the sustainability of the university and supports provided to students. 

The higher education landscape is changing and the push toward 4-year institutions is decreasing. Students are increasingly looking at the cost of a college degree and calculating the cost/benefit analysis of attending. This has led to some asking for more associate and technical degree programs. We are a 4-year accredited university with the option for associates degrees and some masters programs but are continuing to look at improving our professional degree programs while also offering comprehensive general education to bachelor's degree students.
